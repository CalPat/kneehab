**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Type:** Minor (philosophy shift — rigid protocol → intuitive movement; gate removal)

## Driver

User self-report, two items:
1. **"Bellemans protocol is never followed — too onerous."** The rigid stationary-bike prescription (fixed seat height at 15–20° flexion, 10–12 min, hard stop 15 min, "Zone 2 ride doesn't replace it") was a compliance failure. It sat as the *default* Collagen Block loading option and the Cardio block opener, so the highest-friction item was the most-prescribed.
2. **Remove the June 17 decision point.** The 2026-06-17 standing-protocol reassessment was a hard dated gate. User wants it gone.

## What changed

### Change 1 — Bellemans Bike protocol dropped (replaced with casual movement)

The **principle** stays (Bellemans: avascular cartilage is fed by cyclic compressive loading → fluid exchange). The **rigid implementation** is gone. New default: *take collagen, then go about your day — a walk or an easy bike ride within the synthesis window.* No seat height, no hard stop, no timer. A commute, a dog walk, or an easy spin all count.

**Physiology note:** nothing is lost. Cyclic compressive loading is the active ingredient, and casual walking/cycling supplies it. The rigid protocol's specificity (exact flexion angle, 15-min ceiling) was always a marginal-returns refinement; trading it for a stimulus that actually gets done is net-positive for cartilage nutrition.
