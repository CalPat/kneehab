# kneehab v14.8 — no load, no dose

**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Type:** medium (live prescription removed across 7 sites)

## The ruling

**The before-bed rest-day collagen dose is gone.** It was never defensible, and the
program already contained the argument against it. Supplements page,
Non-Negotiable 03:

> A qualifying mechanical load event must follow within 45–60 min. Collagen
> without mechanical stimulus is metabolized as general protein — not directed
> to connective tissue.

The rest-day instruction told you to take it with no load at all. The page
contradicted itself.

**Mechanism:** the nocturnal GH pulse *amplifies* collagen synthesis. It does not
*direct* peptides to a tissue — mechanical strain does that, and nothing is
straining at 2 AM. A systemic amino-acid pool with no strain signal has nowhere
to go.

**Second contradiction:** the same page also rules *"no food 90 min before sleep —
insulin response suppresses the GH pulse."* The old card waved this away with
"a small collagen dose is not a meal," while elsewhere the program notes
collagen **is** mildly insulinogenic (which is why it is taken inside the eating
window). Two rules, opposite directions, same page.

**Third:** the day it was written for no longer exists. The rule dates from the
Day A / Day B era. Under v14 the **Opener runs 7/7** and **Rest day carries 90s
mobility holds plus a walk** — there is no day without a qualifying loading
event.

## Standing rule

> **Dose to a load, or skip it.** Collagen is aimed by strain, not by the clock.

Consistent with v14.1 (*the window is an amplifier, not a gate*) — that rule says a
**load without the window** is still worth doing. This one says the inverse is not:
a **dose without a load** buys nothing.
