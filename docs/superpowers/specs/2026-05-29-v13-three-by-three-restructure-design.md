# v13.0 — Three-by-Three Restructure

**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Driver:** Cal — months pain-free, KB-loading well, leg/knee strength dialed, cardio inconsistent
**Predecessor:** v12.04 (Weekly Cardio Total target added to Daily Habits)
**Change size:** Major — restructures the entire weekly framework (justifies v13.0, not v12.05)

---

## 1. Driver

v12 progression accomplished its goals: knee rehab established, leg and knee strength built, upper-body base in place. Two unresolved gaps:

1. **Cardio remained informal.** v12.04 added a 180 min/week tracking target to Daily Habits, but no protected slots existed in the program. Cardio competed against Day A/B for the same days. In practice, knee-strength sessions won; cardio days were skipped.
2. **Day A/Day B differentiation was thin.** After v12.03 (Day B Knee Maintenance cut) and v12.04 (warm-up not yet daily), the two day templates carried similar lifts with light variation. The "which day is this" decision added no real value.

Cal proposed: 6 hr/week training target, 1 hr/day with one rest day. 50/50 split between strength and cardio gives 3 strength + 3 cardio + 1 rest. Combine Day A and Day B into a single template. Add a daily warm-up that absorbs mobility, core, and the right-side glute med pattern work into a high-frequency low-intensity background commitment.

v13.0 is a structural restructure, not an additive change. It supersedes Day A and Day B as named entities and replaces them with a single Strength session repeated 3x per cycle.

---

## 2. Philosophy

**Strength and cardio share equal status.** Each gets ~3 hr/week protected. Cardio is no longer "do when you can."

**Cardio is intensity-agnostic.** No zone gating, no HR prescription. Log time. Mix easy and tempo as the day allows. Consensus number (180 min/week target) carries forward from v12.04.

**Single strength template.** No A/B differentiation. Every Strength session is the same loaded work. Eliminates the "which day" mental tax. Each muscle pattern gets 3x/week frequency automatically.

**Daily warm-up = real training.** ~16 min done 6 of 7 days. Absorbs mobility, anterior core, lateral core, glute med, adductor, anti-rotation, scap retraction. Daily exposure to right-side glute med pattern work (v12.01 thread) — currently only 2x/week on Day A.

**Long-hold mobility consolidates on Rest day.** Pigeon, Lizard, Couch Stretch all share the viscoelastic-creep mechanism. Grouped on Rest day where their long-hold character fits. Removed from daily warm-up where they were redundant or overdosed.

**No phasing.** Per CLAUDE.md, the program evolves continuously; this is the v12.04 → v13.0 state.

## 3. Rotation Sequence

**7-session cycle:** Strength → Cardio → Strength → Cardio → Strength → Cardio → Rest

| # | Session | Plyo | Loaded time | Total (incl. warm-up) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strength | Jump Rope | ~52 min | ~68 min |
| 2 | Cardio | — | 60 min | ~76 min |
| 3 | Strength | KB Swing | ~52 min | ~68 min |
| 4 | Cardio | — | 60 min | ~76 min |
| 5 | Strength | — | ~47 min | ~63 min |
| 6 | Cardio | — | 60 min | ~76 min |
| 7 | Rest | — | ~35 min | ~35 min |

**Properties:**
- Each Strength session bracketed by Cardio
- Plyo lands on 2 of 3 Strength sessions. Same-exercise spacing: 7 days between Jump Rope exposures (and between KB Swing exposures) — well-recovered. Cross-exercise spacing: 48h between Jump Rope (vertical impact) and KB Swing (hip-hinge, minimal GRF) — different stimuli, acceptable per v12.0 rationale
- Rest follows highest cumulative load (3 Strength + 3 Cardio + 6 Warm-ups)
- Activity Day (ski, hike, ride) absorbs any Cardio slot — does not require schedule adjustment
- Days of week are not specified — Cal maps cycle positions to calendar

**Weekly total commitment:** ~7:45–8:15 active (depending on optional finisher uptake). Base math: 151 min Strength loaded + 180 min Cardio + 96 min Warm-up (6 × 16) + 35 min Rest = 462 min = 7:42.

## 6. Cardio Session

**60 min intentional cardio per session, 3 sessions per cycle = 180 min/week (matches v12.04 target).**

| Component | Dose | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Warm-Up | ~16 min | Same as Strength sessions |
| Bellemans Bike (default opener) | 10–12 min working · hard stop 15 min · 15–20° flexion at bottom | Cyclic cartilage stim — irreplaceable for knee cartilage health |
| Main aerobic | 45–50 min · modality flexes | Ride · Ruck · Hike · Row · Swim |
| OR Activity Day | Variable · 60–180+ min | Ski · Hike · Ride — absorbs Cardio slot entirely |

**No zone gating.** Log time. Intensity flexes. If it felt aerobic, it counts. v12.04 framing carries forward — BBM-flavored, cross-source consensus on the 180 min/week number.

**Sequencing rule:** dedicated cardio after strength on same day, or 6+ hours separated. Never immediately before a Strength session.

## 10. Volume Tradeoffs and Right-Side Gating

### Volume bump from v12.04

| Item | v12.04 frequency | v13.0 frequency | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| KB Front Rack Squat | 2x/week (Day A) | 3x/week | +50% |
| Step Down | 2x/week (Day A, with rotation skip) | 3x/week | +50–100% |
| ISO Step-Up | rotation choice | 3x/week | +200% |
| Split Squat 2s | rotation choice | 3x/week | +200% |
| Knee unilateral total | ~6 exposures/week | ~9 exposures/week | +50% |

This is a moderate jump. Justified by:
- Cal months pain-free
- KB-loading well, plateaued on bodyweight progressions
- Strength research (Schoenfeld) supports 3x/week per pattern over 1–2x for adaptation

### Right-side gating preserved

All knee unilateral work remains right-side bodyweight gated until reassessment criterion clears: **clean 30s right single-leg stance with no compensation** (v12.01 thread, lives in Daily Habits SL Standing Micro-Dose).

Items gated to right-side bodyweight:
- Step Down (slant board)
- ISO Step-Up
- Split Squat 2s

Left side may progress to loaded variations per 2×2 rule. Right side holds at bodyweight until reassessment passes.

### Progression rule (universal)

**2×2 rule applies per exercise.** ≤2/10 pain during AND morning after, two consecutive sessions → pull the next lever. Pain >2/10 → hold current level one week. One lever at a time.

For new movements (Cossack, Pallof): start conservative. Bodyweight Cossack 3×6, light-band Pallof 3×8. Progress in single-step increments.

### Stop-rule for plyo

Knee pain or effusion → drop one plyo phase, hold 2 weeks. Plyo phasing inherited from v12.0:
- Phase 1 (wk 1–3): rope only
- Phase 2 (4–7): box jump-up
- Phase 3 (8–11): broad jumps + bilateral drop jump 12"
- Phase 4 (12+): SL hops + drop jump 18–24"

## 12. Reversibility

If volume proves excessive (knee symptoms return, generalized fatigue, missed sessions):

| Symptom | First lever | Second lever |
|---|---|---|
| Knee symptoms during Strength | Drop Split Squat (most-overlapped with Step Down) | Drop ISO Step-Up (preserve eccentric Step Down + bilateral KB FR Squat) |
| Generalized fatigue / poor recovery | Drop optional finishers entirely | Cut Strength sessions from 3x → 2x per cycle, add a Rest position |
| Plyo intolerance | Drop one phase (per v12.0 rule), hold 2 weeks | Remove plyo from program until reassessment |
| Right-side regression | Tighten gating — remove right-side load entirely, drop to bodyweight on left | Reduce Strength to 2x per cycle on right side |
| Cardio target consistently unmet | Re-examine 60 min per session realism — drop to 45 min × 3 = 135 min/week (between WHO 150 floor and AHA range) | |

The structural decisions (single template, daily warm-up, 3+3+1 rotation) are reversible by re-introducing A/B differentiation and moving warm-up items back into session core blocks. Nothing in v13 is structurally locked.

## Appendix A — Expert Review Decisions

Initial v13 draft was stress-tested against five expert lenses: Peter Attia (longevity / Zone 2 / VO2 max), Barbell Medicine (evidence-based strength), Keith Baar (connective tissue mechanobiology), Johan Bellemans (cartilage cyclic loading), and a generalist kinesiology lens (biomechanics / motor control).

| Source | Critique | v13 response |
|---|---|---|

| BBM | Too many corrective-exercise additions for someone months pain-free | **Cossack Squat and Pallof Press removed.** Strength session reduced from 15 to 13 items |
| Kinesiology | Drop landing for deceleration | **Acknowledged — already in v12.0 plyo phasing (Phase 3 bilateral drop jump 12"), inherited by v13** |
| BBM | No barbell, no bench | **Acknowledged as out-of-scope for v13. Cal is beginner to weight training; KB-first progression is the v13 stance. Deferred to future versions.** |
