# kneehab v13.1 — retire Side Plank; add Suitcase Carry, Standing Hip Abduction, and Mǎbù to Daily Warm-Up

**Date:** 2026-06-18
**Type:** Medium (Warm-Up block composition change — 1 item retired, 3 added)

## Driver

User self-report: Side Plank, adduction, and Copenhagen are "annoying to do" but acknowledged as important. Request narrowed over the conversation to: (1) add **Mǎbù (horse stance)** and **Suitcase Carry** to the daily warm-up, (2) keep Copenhagen if still needed, (3) replace Side Plank with the carry, (4) decide whether the abduction component should be absorbed by the carry or kept standalone.

## Key decisions (the clinical reasoning)

**Side Plank w/ Abduction was two stimuli welded together** — anti-lateral-flexion (the side-plank hold) + glute-med *prime-mover* isolation (the top-leg abduction). They split cleanly:

- **Suitcase Carry replaces the anti-lateral-flexion role.** The carry trains QL/obliques anti-lateral-flexion + glute med as a *stabilizer* (resisting pelvic drop on the loaded-stance side). More specific to the v12.01 standing/Trendelenburg pattern than a static floor hold — it is essentially loaded standing. This is the item the user actually finds tolerable.
- **Standing Hip Abduction preserves the glute-med isolation.** The carry only trains glute med as a *stabilizer*; the abduction trains it as a *prime mover* in the frontal plane. For the v12.01 right-side **inhibition** thread, prime-mover activation is the targeted tool — and the abduction component was the program's *only* dedicated frontal-plane glute-med isolation. Folding it into the carry would have lost it. Kept off the floor (standing) to honour the "less annoying" intent. **Band is the default** (standing gravity barely loads the moving leg) — and standing adds a bonus stance-leg pelvic-level stabilizer that *is* the v12.01 fault. **No band → side-lying abduction** (bodyweight loads the top-leg glute med hard, ~70–80% MVIC; loses the stance-leg component but keeps the prime-mover activation, and doubles as a clean L/R strength check).
- **Copenhagen kept.** Mǎbù loads adductors at *length as stabilizers* (low tension); Copenhagen is a high-tension adductor strengthener and the adductor magnus supports the medial knee (relevant to the right medial joint-line thread). Mǎbù does **not** replace it.
- **Mǎbù (horse stance) added** as a genuinely new stimulus: isometric quad loading (Baar/Rio collagen window) + adductor length + postural isometric. Knees-permitting.

## Carry load guidance (baked into the card)

- **~35 lb / 16 kg is a solid warm-up dose.** The warm-up carry is *activation*, not a max lift — go heavier only if resisting the lean is trivially easy.
- **Keep it TRUE suitcase — one side only.** A bell in the other hand cancels the asymmetry (35 + 20 = only 15 lb of net lateral demand → ~60% of the stimulus gone; it becomes a farmer carry). The asymmetry *is* the exercise. Switch sides between lengths.
- Heavy two-bell carries (farmer / front-rack) belong in the optional **Strength finisher**, which already exists (`sS_opt`) and is unchanged.
