**Type:** Minor (block removal, no new exercises)
**Predecessor:** v12.02 (drop Scap Push-Up, fold scap protraction into Down Dog)

## Driver

Rio/Baar daily-isometric protocols are evidence-based for *active patellar tendinopathy*, not for asymptomatic maintenance. User is months pain-free. The "half-dose maintenance" framing (SL Wall Sit 3×20s on Day B vs Day A's 5×20–30s) was worst-of-both — sub-therapeutic dose + decision overhead. Tib Raise was triple-exposed (Daily Habits + Day A + Day B). Copenhagen on Day A's glute block is sufficient for asymptomatic adductor health.

Recovery is part of adaptation. Day B was loading the knee every other day; the lower body never got a full off day. v12.03 fixes that.

## Reversibility

If patellar tendon symptoms ever return: add SL Wall Sit back as targeted intervention (3 × 30–45s at the affected angle, Rio protocol) rather than baseline ritual. Easy to restore.

## Stop-Signs

- Patellar tendon pain returning under Day A loading
- Step Down or Split Squat pain emerging
- Cumulative knee stiffness building over the week
- Right-side compensation pattern worsening

Any of these → reintroduce targeted SL Wall Sit at therapeutic dose, then re-evaluate.
