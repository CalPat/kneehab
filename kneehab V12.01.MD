# kneehab v12.01 — Standing Protocol Added to Daily Habits

**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Type:** Minor (Daily Habits additions, no structural program change)
**Predecessor:** v12.0 (KB loading, plyo primer, loaded carry, science-based trim)

## Clinical Driver

Right knee medial joint-line discomfort under prolonged standing. Resolves quickly with rest. No structural injury markers (no swelling, no catching, no rest pain, no training provocation).

**Mechanism**: right glute med inhibition (v11.95 diagnosis) produces *two* downstream compensation patterns from the same root cause:

| Context | Compensation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Cued single-leg load (training) | Knee valgus | Lateral patellar tracking — v11.95 cues addressed this |
| Unconscious sustained load (standing) | Trendelenburg lurch — trunk drifts laterally over loaded leg | Varus moment at right knee → medial compartment compression |

The v11.95 cue work fixed the trained pattern. The unconscious pattern remained — and surfaces as medial joint-line discomfort across a standing day.

## What v12.01 Adds

### Standing Posture Check (Daily Habits, ⭐ priority)
**Every 20–30 min during long standing**. Three checks, ~5s each:
1. Head centered over hips (no lateral lurch)
2. Weight even on both feet (feel through both heels and balls of feet equally)
3. Light right glute engagement (sustained baseline tone, not a flash)

Set a timer or attach to existing cues (coffee, between tasks). Discipline is the dose.

### Right-Side SL Standing Micro-Dose (Daily Habits, ⭐ priority)
**10–20s × 5–10 per day**. Standing normally, lift left foot, active right glute med holds pelvis level — no drop on unloaded side, no lurch on loaded side. Ribs stacked directly over pelvis.

**Cue check**: can you feel right glute med working? If right adductor or QL is doing the work, that's the v11.95 compensation pattern firing — relax, reset, find glute med specifically.

**Quality > duration**: 10s clean beats 30s compensated.

**Progression**: 10s → 20s → eyes closed → slight knee bend → with arm reach.

**Reassessment criterion**: clean 30s right SL stance with no compensation = right glute med inhibition resolving.

## Why Daily Habits Was the Right Home

- Training is ~50 min/day of cued, perfect loading.
- Standing days are 6+ hours of unconscious loading.
- Compensation patterns live in the unconscious window — that's where the intervention has to happen.
- Daily Habits is the section designed for opportunistic, no-recovery-cost, frequency-driven items. Both new items fit.

## What Did Not Change

- Day A (Mobility, Plyo Primer, Core, Glute, Dead Hang, Knee Block) — unchanged
- Day B (Mobility, Core, Knee Maintenance, Upper Body, Suitcase Carry) — unchanged
- Day R, Activity, Collagen Block — unchanged
- v12.0 KB additions (Front Rack Squat, Suitcase Carry, Plyo Primer) and rotations/cuts stand
- v11.95 right-side cueing on Step Down · Hip Abduction · SL Glute Bridge · Bird Dog — unchanged
- **Right-side gating on Step Down · Split Squat · SL Wall Sit stands.** The standing-day finding *validates* the gating, not relaxes it. Loading these unilateral patterns while unconscious compensation is still active would entrench medial compartment stress.

## Stop-Signs (unchanged from v12.0)

- Medial joint-line pain stops resolving with rest
- Catching, locking, giving way
- Pain at rest or at night
- Visible swelling
- Pain provoked by training (especially squat or step down)

Any of these → PT, escalate.
