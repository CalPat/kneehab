# kneehab v13.41 — Depth Gate + 24-Hour Response Rule

**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Type:** Minor (.01) — one new gating mechanism, one new Rest-day item, one free exercise upgrade.
**Supersedes:** v13.4 (RPE → RIR + observable set terminators)

---

## Why this version exists

A deep bodyweight squat on 2026-08-13 (run as a Cossack-squat limiter test) provoked **sharp anterolateral knee pain at the inferior-patellar level** — in line with the bottom of the kneecap, just lateral.

Presentation:

| Feature | Observation | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | At depth, and **delayed** after standing | Reactive, not a mechanical block |
| Character | Sharp at depth → discomfort after | Irritative response |
| Resolution | **Fully gone in ~60 min** | **Low irritability** |
| Locking / catching / giving way | None | No structural signal |

**Conclusion:** end-range patellofemoral compression limit. Mild, positional, self-resolving — not an injury, but a real depth ceiling.

### The delay is the important part

Pain arriving *after* the session means the **v13.4 in-session terminators cannot see it**. 2 RIR, first grinding rep, first compensated rep — all assume the signal lands during the set. For this knee it lands hours later.

> "Nothing hurt during the session" is no longer evidence the session was fine.

That gap is what v13.41 closes.

---

## Changes

### 1. Depth is now a gated, progressed variable — `S`

Double-progression logic (v13.4) applied to **range** instead of load.

- **KB Front Rack Squat** — box or bench, set at a height confidently pain-free (start at or just above parallel, deliberately conservative). Touch-and-go, don't sit and rest.
- **Split Squat** — pad or block under the back knee to cap descent, same principle.
- **Progression:** hold the height for a **full clean cycle** (all three `S` sessions, no sharp pain, no next-morning elevation) → drop the box one notch.
- **Mǎbù (`W`)** — pinned at or above the current box height. It's a sustained isometric, so it sits *above* the capped depth, never at it.

**Do not probe for the edge.** Repeatedly loading to the limit of a reactive patellofemoral joint is how a one-off becomes a six-week problem. Start conservative and let the knee earn range back.

Objective terminator, not a judgment call at rep 8 under fatigue — same design philosophy as v13.4.

### 2. 24-Hour Response Rule — governing gate above the in-session terminators

Standard load-management control for reactive patellofemoral and tendon presentations.

Each morning after an `S` day, before moving properly:
- First **ten steps** out of bed
- One slow **sit-to-stand** from a chair
- Note anterior knee **0–10**

| Result | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Back to baseline within 24h | Load was acceptable | Repeat; consider lowering the box after a clean cycle |
| Still elevated next morning | That session was too much | Raise the box, hold, no progression this cycle |

**Escalate out of programming** (in-person eval) on: swelling, night or rest pain, giving way, locking or catching, or inability to fully straighten.

### 3. SL Glute Bridge → psoas lock — `S`

Free upgrade, zero added time. Pull the **free** knee above 90° and press both hands into it isometrically for the duration of each rep.

- **Primary:** glute max + hamstring (bridging side); psoas / iliacus + rectus femoris (locked side)
- **Compensations:** working-side hip dropping as you press — pelvis stays level; existing pelvis-drop terminator still ends the set
- **Regression:** hands light, free knee at 90°
- **Progression:** hands off — hold the knee up with hip flexors alone

One exercise now trains loaded hip extension on one side and end-range hip flexion on the other.

### 4. Frog Rock → `R` (Hips Tight category)

Loaded adductor length at end range with **zero knee compression** — which is precisely why it belongs on Rest day rather than in a loaded block, and why it's the correct entry point regardless of whether the limiter turns out to be groin or knee.

`8 slow reps · 3s pause at end range`

- **Primary:** adductor magnus + brevis, hip capsule, hip internal rotators at length
- **Compensations:** lumbar flexion at the back end (stop the instant the low back rounds — motion comes from the hip, not the spine); knees splayed wider than the hips tolerate (groin should feel stretch, knee should feel nothing)
- **Regression:** narrower knees, shorter rock, no pause
- **Progression:** wider knees → 5s pause → light band around the thighs

First rung of the Cossack ladder.

## Considered and rejected

Reviewed against the Movesmethod YouTube material (2026-08-13). Documented so it isn't re-litigated.

| Item | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Nordic curls** | **No.** Kneeling load through the patellae + very large knee-flexor torque ≈ worst-case pattern for a PFPS history. Kickstand RDL already covers eccentric posterior chain, hip-dominantly. |
| **Anterior-pelvic-tilt correction** | **No.** Weak evidence base; APT varies enormously in asymptomatic people, and the wall test measures a motor task, not a structural finding. |
| **Standalone daily hip-mobility routine** | **No.** `W` already runs 18 min before every `S` and `C`. A parallel routine dilutes a working program rather than adding to it. |
| **Cossack squat** | **Excluded indefinitely.** Demands exactly the deep loaded knee flexion this version caps. Entry rung (Frog Rock) went to `R` instead. |

### Reviewed — already covered, no change needed

- **Active hip rotation** → Shin-Box Get-Ups already does this (v13.3 replaced static 90/90).
- **Active end-range hip flexion** → the `W` Hip Flexor slot is Tuck L-Sit / Hanging Knee Raise, already loaded and active, not a passive stretch.

### What the Movesmethod material got right

- *"A tight hamstring is not a short hamstring, it is a protected hamstring"* — correct, mainstream. Perceived tightness is largely stretch tolerance, a nervous-system output.
- **Neurodynamic differentiation** (slump / chin-tuck test) — genuinely diagnostic, the best item in their catalogue. Ruled out here.
- **Strength through full ROM over passive stretching** — correct, and already the program's position via Mitchell and ATG since v11.

## Findings this version

- **Ankle dorsiflexion: 5 in (~12.7 cm), knee-to-wall.** Above the ~10 cm threshold. **Ruled out as a limiter anywhere** — not Cossack, not squat depth, not Mǎbù, not Step Down. Any depth limit is hip or knee, never ankle. Makes **Kneeling Ankle the lowest-yield item in `W`** — maintenance, not corrective. First candidate slot if one is ever needed.
- **Neural / sciatic contribution: ruled out** (no chin-tuck response).
- **Deep knee flexion: positive** — this version's subject.

## Unchanged

Rotation `S → C → S → C → S → C → R`. All 14 `S` items mandatory, 2 working sets, v13.4 RIR + quality terminators intact. `W` still 12 items. `C` 60 min × 3/cycle. `N` collagen block. `D` daily habits. Foundations unchanged (Baar, Bellemans, Patrick/ATG, Mitchell, San Millán, Attia).

`C` is explicitly **unaffected and encouraged** — flat, high cadence, low resistance. That's Bellemans' cyclic cartilage loading; no grinding climbs or big-gear work while the knee is settling.
